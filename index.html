FAQ

Questions people ask before switching

Do I need an account or internet to use Invora?

No. Invora works fully offline and your data lives on your device. Signing in is optional — it adds automatic cloud backup, phone-to-tablet sync, tracked links and read receipts.

What happens to my invoices if I stop paying for Pro?

Nothing is locked away. Every invoice, client and payment you created stays fully accessible, and you can still view, share and export them. You simply return to the free plan’s monthly limit for new documents.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup file from Settings and restore it on the new device, or sign in and let cloud backup bring everything across automatically — including your logo, templates and photo attachments.

Is it GST compliant?

Invora produces tax invoices with GSTIN, HSN/SAC, place of supply, CGST/SGST/IGST, reverse charge and amount in words, plus tax summaries for filing. Always confirm requirements for your turnover and state with your CA.

Can my clients pay online?

Add your UPI, PayPal, Stripe or any payment link to your business profile and every invoice shows a scan-to-pay QR code and a tappable “Pay online” link.

Does it handle long invoices?

Yes — invoices with dozens of lines flow across pages with a repeated header and page numbers, so totals are never cut off.
